# OneNote AI Journal Template — "My AI Journal"

Instructions for setting up your personal AI Journal in OneNote. Each person on the pilot team should create their own copy.

---

## HOW TO SET UP YOUR JOURNAL

1. Open **OneNote** (app or onenote.com)
2. Create a **New Notebook**: Name it `[Your Name]'s AI Journal`
3. Create the following **Sections** (tabs at the top):
   - Course Notes
   - Prompt Ideas
   - Experiments Log
   - IDP & Goals
   - Meeting AI Notes
   - Resources & Bookmarks
4. Share the link to your notebook on the SharePoint site (optional — keeps everyone connected)

## QUICK-START HABIT GUIDE

Build these habits for maximum AI growth:

| Habit | When | Time |
|-------|------|------|
| Try 1 new prompt per day | During your workday | 5 min |
| Log what you tried | End of day | 3 min |
| Complete 1 course module per week | Any time | 30-60 min |
| Share 1 use case per month | Month end | 10 min |
| Monthly reflection in IDP section | Last Friday of month | 15 min |

**Remember:** The people who get the most out of AI are not the smartest — they're the most curious and consistent.
