## GAMIFICATION OVERVIEW

### Points System

| Activity | Points | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Complete a course or module | +10 | Log in IDP Tracker to claim |
| Earn an AI certification | +50 | Submit via Certs & Badges form |
| Submit an approved prompt | +5 | Approved by AI Guru |
| Submit a use case | +20 | Added to Use Cases Gallery |
| Complete a monthly challenge | +15 | Verified by AI Guru |
| Help a colleague (nominated) | +10 | Someone nominates you |
| Attend a live AI session/workshop | +10 | Track via sign-up list |

### How Points Are Tracked

1. You complete an activity
2. You (or the AI Guru) update your row in the `Leaderboard` SharePoint list
3. The list auto-sorts by Total Points descending
4. Displayed on the Community page using the List web part

**Automation (Phase 2):** Power Automate can automatically add points when you submit a form or complete a linked course.

### CHALLENGES BANK (12 months of challenges)

**Month 1 — "First Contact"**
Use Copilot for a real work task for the first time this month. Document:
- What you asked it to do
- The result (paste it)
- What you changed or improved
- Time estimate: how long would it have taken you manually?

**Month 2 — "The Promptsmith"**
Take a prompt from the Prompt Library and improve it. Submit the improved version with an explanation of what you changed and why the new version is better.

**Month 3 — "Meeting Makeover"**
Use Teams Copilot in at least 3 meetings this month. Submit a before/after comparison: how did your meeting follow-up process change?

**Month 4 — "Research Rockstar"**
Use Copilot Researcher to create a 1-page research brief on a topic relevant to your work. Share the brief with your team and post it to the Use Cases gallery.

**Month 5 — "Data Whisperer"**
Use Copilot Analyst to analyze a real work dataset or document. What insight did you find that you wouldn't have spotted manually?

**Month 6 — "Email Superpower"**
Draft every external or complex email using Copilot for one full week. Track: How many emails did you write? Average time saving per email? One example you're proud of.

**Month 7 — "Teach It Forward"**
Teach a colleague how to use one Copilot feature they haven't tried before. Document what you taught them and what their reaction was. Bonus points if they submit their own use case!

**Month 8 — "Prompt of the Month"**
Create the best new prompt submitted this month. Judged on: creativity, usefulness, clarity, and real-world applicability. Winner gets a featured spot on the Home page.

**Month 9 — "Agent Builder"**
Build a basic Copilot Agent (even a simple one with 5 FAQs). It doesn't have to be perfect — just working. Document what problem it solves and who it helps.

**Month 10 — "Certification Sprint"**
Complete at least one Microsoft AI or Copilot certification this month. Earn your +50 cert points plus the +15 challenge bonus.

**Month 11 — "AI Use Case of the Year"**
Submit your best use case of the year. The one that saved the most time, solved the most important problem, or helped the most people. The top 3 get featured in the Year in Review.

**Month 12 — "AI Year in Review"**
Reflect on your AI journey this year. Complete a reflection in your OneNote journal and share a highlight with the team: what's the most valuable thing AI has done for you this year?

## AI CHAMPION RECOGNITION

At the end of each month, the AI Guru selects an **AI Champion** based on:
- Highest activity and points
- Best use case or prompt submitted
- Outstanding community contribution (helping others)

The AI Champion gets:
- Featured on the Community page with their photo and a quote
- Named in the company newsletter or Teams channel
- Special badge on their leaderboard entry
- Maybe a small recognition gift (coffee, voucher — your call!)

## PILOT KICKOFF TIPS

For your first month with the pilot group:

1. **Start everyone at Copilot Curious** — no exceptions, even you
2. **Run Month 1 challenge: "First Contact"** — low bar, just try it
3. **Be the first to submit a use case** — model the behaviour you want
4. **Share your own experiment log** (from OneNote) in the Teams channel — vulnerability builds trust
5. **Weekly 5-minute update in Teams**: "This week I used Copilot for X and it saved me Y"
6. **Celebrate small wins loudly** — every "I saved 20 minutes" matters

The goal is to make it feel fun and safe to experiment, not a performance review.
